Practice Specialties

OUR NICHE & SERVICES

Comprehensive compliance and advisory solutions tailored for private, public, and listed entities.

Pillar I

Corporate Secretarial & ROC Compliance

  • Entity Incorporation: Private/Public Companies, Section 8, LLPs, and Business Conversions.
  • Critical Corporate Actions: Share capital restructuring, board room reconstitutions, registered office shifting, and charge management.
  • Exit & Strike-Off: Fast-track corporate closures & strike-offs.
  • Regulatory E-Filing & XBRL: MCA compliance filings, XBRL conversion & submission.
  • Governance & Statutory Records: Board/AGM meeting administration, statutory register maintenance, and Secretarial Standards adherence.
Pillar II

Capital Markets & SME IPOs

  • SME IPO Advisory: Structuring companies for public market capital raising.
  • End-to-End Management: Steering companies through the complete SME IPO process.
  • DRHP & RHP Filings: Drafting Prospectus documents with regulatory accuracy.
  • Regulatory Coordination: Interfacing with Merchant Bankers, SEBI, stock exchanges & registrar bodies.
  • Post Listing Compliances: Ongoing post-IPO statutory reporting.
Pillar III

Listing Regulation & SEBI Compliance

  • SEBI Regulations: Strict adherence to SEBI LODR, SAST, and PIT codes.
  • Stock Exchange Submissions: Timely disclosures and filing workflows.
  • SDD Compliance: Structured Digital Database setup & maintenance.
  • E-Voting Procedures: Managing shareholder voting & scrutinizer roles.
  • Investor Relations: Corporate governance policies and investor interface.
Pillar IV

Audit & Legal Drafting

  • Secretarial Audits: Comprehensive independent statutory secretarial audits.
  • Internal Governance Audits: Operational compliance verification.
  • Legal Drafting: Shareholder agreements, commercial contracts, and deeds.
  • Audit Coordination: Liaison with statutory auditors & legal counsels.
